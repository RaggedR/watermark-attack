Image Datasets for Watermark Attack Evaluation
================================================

Location: /Volumes/One Touch/diffusion-watermarking/datasets/
Download: python scripts/download_datasets.py

## DIV2K — Natural Photos (control domain)

Source: https://data.vision.ee.ethz.ch/cvl/DIV2K/
Count:  900 images (800 train + 100 validation)
Size:   ~3.3 GB
Format: PNG, ~2K resolution (various aspect ratios)
Content: High-res natural photos (people, landscapes, architecture, etc.)
Purpose: Out-of-distribution control — tests if attack params generalise
         beyond AI-generated content.

## DiffusionDB — SD-Generated Images (target domain)

Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/poloclub/diffusiondb (random_1k subset)
Count:  1,000 images
Size:   ~0.5 GB
Format: PNG, 512x512
Content: Stable Diffusion 1.x outputs with diverse prompts
Purpose: Closest proxy for SynthID's target domain (AI-generated images).
         Attack params optimised here are most likely to transfer.

## Domain Comparison

Results are split by dataset to answer: "Do optimal attack parameters
differ between natural photos and AI-generated images?"
If they converge → high confidence the attack generalises to SynthID.
If they diverge → need domain-specific attack tuning.
